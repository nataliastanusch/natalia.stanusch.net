**Natalia Stanusch** is a researcher at AI Forensics, a PhD Candidate in media studies at the University of Amsterdam, and a member of the Digital Methods Initiative.

Contact: natalia[at]stanusch[dot]net

Natalia’s research foci meet at a crossing of critical data and algorithm studies, science and technology studies, and digital visual culture.

In her work for the NGO [AI Forensics](https://aiforensics.org), she focuses on mixed methods approaches to conducting empirical and data-driven investigations, specializing in algorithmic auditing in the platform context and the online engagements with (generative) artificial intelligence.

She is an author of book chapters, articles, and video essays which range in scope from methodological interventions to original empirical and theoretical piecies.

She was an academic coordinator for a European non-profit [DataEthics.eu](https://DataEthics.eu), a research lead for [‘Slow AI/Esoteric AI’](https://aixdesign.co/projects/esoteric-ai) project run by the collective AIxDESIGN & an author of a blog on art and technology at [hashtagart.blog](https://hashtagart.blog/).

Natalia is currently pursuing a PhD in Media Studies from the Amsterdam School for Cultural Analysis, University of Amsterdam. She holds a MA in New Media and Digital Culture from the University of Amsterdam and a double-major BA in Communications and Media Studies as well as Art History from John Cabot University. During her PhD, she joined the [Summer Doctoral Program](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/study/summer-doctoral-programme/sdp-alumni-gallery/#sdp2025california) run by the Oxford Internet Institute & UC Berkeley School of Information.
