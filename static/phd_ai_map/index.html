Legend

Node kinds (icon inside the circle)

field — a domain of practice / research area (circle)
stage — a step in the production pipeline (a rail station pill)
issue — a controversy / contested matter (circle)
incident — a documented event (circle)
response — regulation / pushback / mitigation (circle)
Non-stage nodes are circles with their label below. In the pipeline domain, stages are stations on a bold horizontal rail and the other nodes are small stop lozenges chained on thin branch lines under their station (the branch is structure, not a link).

Edge kinds (coloured by source domain)

pipeline — the production spine (solid, bold)
cause — cascading consequence (very light)
feeds — resource / data input (light dashed)
response — regulation / pushback (long dash)

I/O bars — left bar = inputs, right bar = outputs

Each domain is a rectangle carrying two vertical coloured bars. The left bar is its input bus; the right bar is its output bus. Every cross-domain arrow leaves through a right bar and arrives at a left bar. A domain drawn inside another is a nested sub-domain (it keeps its own bars and flows).
The small notches on a bar are ports, coloured by the other endpoint's domain — so each bar reads as a manifest of what flows in or out. Long right-to-left swooping arcs are feedback loops.
At overview, cross-domain flows are bundled: one thick translucent trunk per domain pair (width grows with the number of links). Tap a trunk to list its member links; tap a node to see its individual cascade. The faint tinted bubbles (ellipses) are thematic clusters of domains — their name sits on the bubble's top rim.
Zoom is semantic: far out each domain shows one big label and its trunks; zoom in and the label travels to its header as the node detail fades up.

Focus mode (tap a node)

⟵ upstream = what caused/enabled it — drawn dashed & cooler.
⟶ downstream = what it leads to — drawn solid & brightened.
Everything else dims. Tap cause/effect rows in the panel to walk the loops. Tap background or press Esc / Back to exit.

◎ Research lens

A zoom-independent focus on the researcher's PhD nodes: they stay fully visible at every zoom (gold ring; far out, gold beacons mark where the threads live), their full cascades brighten above the map, everything else dims, and the research threads show at the bottom.
Tap a node while the lens is on to refine to that node's own cascade — Esc/Back steps back to the lens first, then off.

✳ Chaos mode

Toggles the bundling off: every individual link — including the inter-domain flows normally hidden inside a trunk — is drawn at once, faintly, at all zooms, above the boxes. The trunks disappear; the raw web of arrows is the point. Entering focus, I/O, a trunk or the research lens temporarily takes over; leaving restores chaos. Only the chaos button turns it off (Esc won't).
